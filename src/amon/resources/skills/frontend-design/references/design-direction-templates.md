# Design Direction Templates

## 1. Minimal Premium SaaS

Best for enterprise apps, B2B SaaS, and productivity tools.

- Generous whitespace
- Near-white background with subtle surface contrast
- Light borders
- Very subtle elevation
- Unified control height
- Medium-large radius
- Gentle hover states
- Clear but restrained focus rings
- Priority: readability and consistency

## 2. Bold Editorial

Best for marketing sites, portfolios, and content-heavy sites.

- Strong typographic hierarchy
- High contrast
- Generous negative space
- Asymmetric layouts with intentional imbalance
- Grid-breaking elements
- Minimal color palette
- Sharp, geometric shapes
- Priority: visual impact and memorability

## 3. Soft and Organic

Best for consumer apps, wellness, and lifestyle brands.

- Rounded corners throughout
- Soft shadows and subtle gradients
- Pastel or muted palette
- Gentle animations
- Curved elements and flowing layouts
- Generous padding
- Soft, blurred backgrounds
- Priority: approachability and comfort

## 4. Dark Neon (Restrained)

Best for developer tools, gaming, and tech products.

- Dark background, but not pure black
- High contrast text
- Accent colors only for CTAs and key states
- Subtle glow on hover
- Minimal borders
- Optional subtle noise texture
- Restrained neon usage
- Priority: focus and sophisticated edge

## 5. Playful and Colorful

Best for consumer apps, children's products, and creative tools.

- Vibrant palette
- Rounded corners
- Micro-animations on hover and interaction
- Generous breathing room
- Friendly geometric illustrations
- Smooth transitions
- High energy but balanced
- Priority: delight and engagement

## Selection Rule

Choose one dominant direction. Do not blend multiple directions until the hierarchy, token system, and user task are already stable.
